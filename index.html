SeatCraft

The seating chart is the hardest puzzle at any wedding. SeatCraft solves it like one: paste the guest list, mark who must sit together and who must not, and get a chart that keeps the peace.

Constraints, not chaos

Must-sit-together pairs, keep-apart pairs, and group tags all feed the solver. It scores every arrangement and keeps improving until nothing better turns up.

Groups stay together

Tag guests - family, college, work, kids - and the solver favors tables where groups land together naturally.

Honest about leftovers

Short on seats? It tells you exactly who doesn't fit, instead of silently squeezing.

Open SeatCraft

Runs entirely in your browser - the guest list never leaves your device. Source on GitHub
